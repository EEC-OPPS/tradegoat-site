The short answer

Pick Jobber if…

you like building quotes in their templates, you want a client hub where customers approve quotes and pay, and you want a big list of add-ons and marketing tools. Expect the bill to grow as you add people.

Pick Housecall Pro if…

most of your work is homeowners, and online booking, review requests and customer financing are what bring the work in. Plans are sold by how many people are on them.

you want to keep sending your own proposal, pay one flat price for the company, see bid against actual while the job runs, and run the office, the field and QuickBooks from one tool.

Where the three really differ

Your proposal, or theirs

Jobber and Housecall Pro both have good quote builders. You build the quote in their format, with their layout.

TradeGOAT starts from the Word or PDF proposal you already send. Upload it, and your finished proposals go out in your format with your logo. The customer signs online. After it's signed, changes become change orders and the original price stays exactly as signed. More on that in Bring your own paperwork.

Knowing if the job is making money while it runs

Jobber has job costing on Grow. Housecall Pro has it on every plan. TradeGOAT gives every plan a live job scorecard: what you bid against what it's actually costing, by cost bucket, while the job is still running. A slipping margin shows up while you can still steer it back. Here's why that matters.

QuickBooks

All three connect to QuickBooks Online: Jobber from Connect up, Housecall Pro from Essentials up, TradeGOAT on every plan. In TradeGOAT, invoices go to QuickBooks, payments come back, and the books' real billing history shows on every job. You can also bring your customers straight in from QuickBooks. How it works: QuickBooks for contractors.

The field

All three give your techs a phone app. In TradeGOAT the tech writes the ticket on the phone — photos, parts, hours — and the customer signs on the glass. The finished PDF emails itself to the customer and the office, and the office can send the bill from that finished ticket. Every ticket stays tied to that piece of equipment for good, so two years later what was done to unit 3 is a page, not a phone call.

Growth adds the maintenance program: service clocks on every unit under contract and a watch for missed visits. Pro adds dispatch and the crew map. Your 14 free days are Pro, so you get all of it from day one.

Where Jobber and Housecall Pro are ahead

Homeowner marketing. Online booking, review requests, email and postcard campaigns and customer financing are big parts of both, and if those are what bring your work in, weigh them. TradeGOAT is built around the work and the money once the call comes in: the proposal, the job, the ticket, the bill and the books.
