Questions owners actually ask

Is this really not per seat?

For the tool itself, yes. Per-seat pricing punishes you for growing and makes you share logins to save money — the thing everybody hates about the big-name tools. TradeGOAT is flat per company within your tier's user count. Hiring your fourth tech costs you nothing. The one exception is the secretary, and we will not bury it: she is priced per person, because she works inside one real mailbox. Adding a technician or another office seat is never an add-on. Adding a second secretary is.

How many secretaries do I get?

Pro includes one — the owner's. Not one for every person in the tool. Each additional secretary is $149/mo and can be given to anyone: your office manager, your service coordinator, whoever actually needs one working alongside them. Prices are before sales tax.

The reason is simple. She is not a chat box everyone shares. She lives inside one person's mailbox and works with exactly that person's reach — your office manager's secretary sees what your office manager sees, and never a dollar a viewer could not see. One of her per person is the only way that works honestly.

What counts as a "user"?

A login. Owner, office, field tech — each person gets their own login with the right view for their role (techs see their work, not your money). Sharing logins is what we're trying to save you from.

What happens when my crew grows past my tier?

You move up one tier and everything comes with you — nothing to migrate, nothing re-set-up. Growth covers up to 10 people; Pro covers up to 25. Past 25 we talk — a shop that size is a different support relationship, and it deserves a real conversation instead of a checkout page.

When do payments actually start?

After your free 14 days of Pro, and you'll know well in advance. You pick a tier, we agree in writing, and only then does billing start — the tool keeps working while we talk. This page is the promise we'll be held to.

What about the AI agents I've heard about?

Add-on specialists (the first, a code-and-licensing reference agent, is in testing now) will price separately as an optional layer. Nothing about agents changes the core tiers above.
