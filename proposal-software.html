How the proposal part works

You don't rebuild your proposal. It starts from yours.

Upload the Word file or PDF you send today. TradeGOAT reads your scope language, terms, warranty and payment schedule, and you confirm every fill-in spot against your own words before anything is saved.

  • One set of numbers, typed once. The price you work up is the proposal you send.
  • Your payment terms, kept as you wrote them, and turned into dollar amounts on the job.
  • Real Word documents out, in your format, with your logo.
  • A proposal can't go out with a blank a customer would see.
  • The customer signs online or on the glass, and signing starts the job.
  • A signed proposal is frozen. Changes after that go on as change orders.
It doesn't stop at the proposal
  • Once it's signed, the same tool dispatches the job to a tech's phone.
  • The crew works from a phone: time clock, service tickets and receipts.
  • Invoices push to QuickBooks and payments read back, so you see what every job made.
