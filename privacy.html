Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026

TradeGOAT is run by TradeGOAT LLC, a Texas company. This page says what information we collect, what we do with it, and what we will never do with it — in plain language, because that is how we write everything.

What we collect on this website

  • Visit counts. We count page visits with an anonymous visitor token so we know which pages people read. It does not identify you, and we do not use advertising trackers.
  • What you give us. If you sign up, we collect what you type: your name, company name, and email address. That is the whole signup by design.

What we collect inside the tool

If your company uses TradeGOAT, the tool stores the business records you put into it — proposals, jobs, customers, tickets, pricing, and anything you connect, such as QuickBooks data. That information belongs to your company. Each company's records are kept in that company's own account, walled off from every other company's.

What we do with it

  • We use your information to run the tool and to answer you when you contact us.
  • If you sign up for the beta or a trial, we will email you about your own account and, occasionally, about what is new in the tool. Reply and tell us to stop, and we stop.
  • We look at anonymous usage patterns to decide what to build next.

What we will never do

  • We do not sell your information. Not your email, not your customer list, not your numbers. Not to anyone, not ever.
  • We do not open your company's records to browse them. Someone at TradeGOAT looks inside your account only when you ask us for help with it.
  • We never email your customers. Anything the tool drafts goes to your own mailbox, and you decide whether it sends.

Facebook, LinkedIn, and other platforms

If you interact with our pages on Facebook or LinkedIn, those platforms handle that data under their own privacy policies. Our Facebook tools are used only to publish our own posts to our own page.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is yours. If you close your account, ask and we will export your records to you and delete them from our systems. Email us and it gets done by a person.

Questions

Write to hello@thetradegoat.com or mail TradeGOAT LLC, PO Box 178, Crandall, TX 75114. A person reads both.
