Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026 · TradeGOAT LLC, a Texas company

Your business records are the most sensitive thing about your business — what you charge, what your jobs make, who your customers are. This page says what we collect, what we do with it, and what we will never do, in plain language. If anything here is unclear, email us and a person will answer.

The commitments, up front:

We will never sell your information. Not your email, not your customer list, not your pricing, not your numbers. Not to data brokers, not to "partners," not to anyone, not ever.

We will never use your information for advertising. No ad pixels on your data, no custom audiences, no lookalike lists, no retargeting built from anything you put in the tool. Your information is never the fuel for anyone's ads, including ours.

We will never read your account beyond what you asked us to fix. When you ask for help, we look at the specific item you asked about — that ticket, that proposal, that sync error — and nothing else. Nobody at TradeGOAT browses customer accounts, and support access ends when your problem does.

The tracking we do is purely internal. We count visits and usage to measure our own work — which pages get read, which features get used. Those measurements stay inside the company and are never shared, sold, or used to profile you anywhere else.

What we collect, and why

On this website

  • Anonymous visit counts. A first-party counter tells us which pages get read. It uses a random token, not your identity, and we do not run advertising trackers of any kind.
  • What you type into the signup form. Your name, company name, and email address. That is the whole signup by design — we do not ask for a card.

Inside the tool

  • Your business records. The proposals, jobs, customers, tickets, pricing, documents, and everything else your company puts in. These exist so the tool can do its job for you, and they belong to your company — full stop.
  • Connected services. If you connect QuickBooks or a mailbox, the tool reads what is needed to do the work you connected it for — matching costs to jobs, filing documents — and nothing more. You choose to connect them, and you can disconnect them whenever you want.
  • Sign-in and security records. Login events and the technical logs it takes to keep accounts secure and the service running.
  • Anonymous usage patterns. Which features get used, in aggregate, so we know what to build next. This is measurement, not surveillance — it is never tied back to you personally for any outside purpose.

When you write to us

If you email us or ask for help, we keep the correspondence so we have the history of what you asked and what we did about it.

Every company's records are walled off

Each company's account is isolated from every other company's at the database level. Your competitors cannot see your numbers, and inside your own company, what each person can see is controlled by the roles you set — money numbers are visible only to the people the owner chooses.

When we would ever access your account

Three cases, and only these:

  • You asked for support. We look at the specific item you asked about and nothing else, as promised above.
  • Setup progress during onboarding. We can see yes/no flags — like whether your documents finished importing — not the content of your records.
  • Something is legally required. See the section on legal requests below.

Who else touches the data

We run on established infrastructure providers, the same way nearly every modern software company does. They store and move data on our behalf and are bound to protect it; they are not permitted to use it for their own purposes:

  • Vercel — hosts the website and application.
  • Supabase — the database where your records live, encrypted at rest.
  • Clerk — handles sign-in. We never see or store your password.
  • Intuit (QuickBooks) and Google — only if you connect them, only for the work you connected them to do.
  • Resend — delivers the tool's transactional email.

Everything moves over encrypted connections. If we ever change providers, this page changes with it.

Your rights over your data

  • It is yours. Ask and we will export your company's records to you in a usable format, anytime, free.
  • Correction. If something about you is wrong, tell us and we fix it.
  • Deletion. Close your account and ask, and we delete your company's records from our live systems promptly, with copies aging out of backups on their normal cycle. A person confirms to you when it is done.
  • The same rights for everyone. We do not make you prove which state or country you live in to be treated decently.

Cookies

We use the cookies required to keep you signed in securely. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Email from us

We email you about your own account, and — if you signed up for it — about what is new in the tool. Every marketing email tells you how to stop getting them, and replying "stop" works too. We never email your customers; anything the tool drafts goes to your own mailbox, and you decide whether it sends.

Legal requests

If law enforcement or a court demands customer data, we require valid legal process, we provide only what the order actually compels, and we tell you it happened unless the law forbids us from doing so.

Where your data lives

Our infrastructure is in the United States. If you use TradeGOAT from outside the U.S., your data is processed here.

Children

TradeGOAT is business software and is not directed at anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

When this policy changes

If we change this policy, the date at the top changes and the new version is posted here. If a change meaningfully affects what happens to your data, we email account owners before it takes effect. We will never change the commitments at the top of this page — those are the deal.
